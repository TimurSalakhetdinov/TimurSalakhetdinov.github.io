## About

Data Scientist & AI Engineer. M.Sc. Management & Data Science candidate at Leuphana University Lüneburg (GPA 1.8, Deutschlandstipendium). Working Student in Analytics at InnoGames, building revenue forecasting infrastructure with time-series foundation models on GCP.

M.Sc. thesis: *Forecasting Free-to-Play Game Revenue with Foundation Models and Agent-Compiled Text Covariates*. A frozen TabPFN-TS-3 checkpoint forecasts daily net revenue three months ahead, and an LLM agent compiles internal planning text into typed covariates. Baseline tier complete (13 systems, 3 titles, 49 monthly forecast origins). Submission December 2026.
