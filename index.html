§ Thesis · Lead Project M.Sc. · InnoGames · submission Dec 2026
Industry partner · InnoGames

Forecasting Free-to-Play Game Revenue with Foundation Models and Agent-Compiled Text Covariates

My company project at InnoGames, also my M.Sc. thesis at Leuphana University, aims to improve 3-month net revenue forecast accuracy and calibrated prediction intervals using a frozen tabular foundation model (TabPFN-TS) with zero task-specific retraining. The research tests whether and how agent-compiled covariates, raw-text features, and embeddings from versioned planning text add measurable value beyond calendar flags, using strict vintage guards and placebo-controlled benchmarks to distinguish genuine document content from calendar timing.

Experiments across 49 monthly origins demonstrate that the frozen foundation forecaster with calendar flags outperforms existing production forecasts across all evaluated games; I am currently writing up the thesis for submission in December 2026.
