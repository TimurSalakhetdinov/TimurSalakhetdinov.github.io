§ 01 About
A brief CV

LLM agent systems for analytics, complemented by time-series forecasting with foundation models.

I develop LLM-based tools for analytics tasks such as building Power BI reports and preparing forecasting inputs. The workflows include explicit approval, validation, and escalation steps so that changes remain controlled and reviewable.

Time-series forecasting is my second specialisation. My M.Sc. thesis at Leuphana University examines whether text-derived covariates improve net revenue forecasts at InnoGames. The research combines time-series foundation models with information extracted from versioned planning documents.

I am completing an M.Sc. in Management & Data Science at Leuphana University as a Deutschlandstipendium scholar, alongside my analytics and agent development work at InnoGames. From April 2027 I am available for full-time AI Engineer and Data Scientist roles in Germany.
